# Fairness audit

For each feature the Opus 5.5 trials failed, this audit checks whether a correct, truth-free model fitted to the 24 observations could meet it (2026-10-21 on conditions c0-c2, extended 2026-10-22 to c0-c4).

| Feature | Finding |
|---|---|
| Standing-wave modulation in front of the seawall (`swmod`) | Fair. Partial reflection of random waves from a structure, with interference between incident and reflected waves, is textbook coastal engineering (Goda; Klopman and van der Meer); only an incoherent treatment of the reflected energy, as in standard spectral models, misses it. The stripes are plainly visible in the observed snapshots: the observed modulation has a median of 0.137, incoherent models give about 0.02, and it was the runs' largest misfit on the given data. Runs 1 and 2 said they saw the stripes and left them out. The wall face must be fitted (520 m); the cell edge at 515 m fails |
| Rip-current speed in the rip region (`R3_rip:u`) | Tolerance 0.15 m/s, set wide because the three observations with a strong rip constrain the law for rip speed against wave forcing only loosely. The reference, the ±2 % draws and the truth-free refits pass, and the reference with its rip-speed coefficient scaled by ×0.6, ×0.8, ×1.2 or ×1.5 passes, with a rip-current error of at most 0.146 m/s and a rip-region direction error of at most 2.6° (limit 4°) ([output](../../results/checks/validation/calibration-recheck.json), entries `kappa_x*`) |
| Direction, breaking fraction and height in the rip and on the bars; height dissipation under storms; height and setup at the wall | Fair: they follow from the bar and channel geometry and the breaking parameters, which the truth-free refits recover from the 24 snapshots (channel position within 0.6 m of the truth from every start) |

I also removed both `swmod` and the rip-current speed from grading. Every Opus trial still failed on fair features: trial 1 at 1.70 (channel geometry: direction, breaking fraction), trial 2 at 1.37 (storm height dissipation, and a fitted tide switch that turns the rip off), and trial 3 at 1.54 (breaking fraction, height and crest height at the wall).

**The added conditions (2026-10-22).** c3, a long-period storm swell, and c4, a near-normal storm, were selected from 123 candidates; candidates that the worst truth-free refit failed were rejected. On the five-condition set the reference passes, ten ±2 % draws pass (worst 0.19-0.78), the four truth-free refits pass (0.29-0.88), and refits of the breaking parameters pass (0.25 and 0.19) both with another standard breaking law (depth-limited `Hmax = gamma d` as in SWAN) and with the reference's own Miche-type `Hmax` ([audit/](audit/)). On 2026-10-26 all of these were re-graded with the current grader, without refitting; every one passes, with the same values except one ±2 % draw (0.49 to 0.57) ([output](../../results/checks/validation/calibration-recheck.json)).
